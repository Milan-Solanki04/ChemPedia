Element group colours

Each fill is shown with the foreground the site would choose for it, and the contrast ratio between them. Every pairing must reach WCAG AA for text; a ratio below 4.5 is a failure and the number turns to tell you so.

Shape and rules

Near-square by default, larger only for a raised panel. The rule below is the project's signature motif: 1px, dotted, in the ink colour.

Components

Rendered by the components the build uses, not by a copy of their markup. Tab through them: focus lands on the search field, then the navigation, and the ring is always visible.

The periodic table

Rendered by the table component from the real element records, in each of its four colour modes, and wired to the legend the way the site wires it. Tab into the first table and use the arrow keys; hover or focus a chip and the group is picked out of the grid; press one and it stays picked out until Escape.

Motion

Short, decelerating, upward. Nothing runs longer than --dur-slow, and the whole layer collapses to nothing under a reduced-motion preference.
