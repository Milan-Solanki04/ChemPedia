Print the periodic table

Everything on this site prints, and nothing here is a file. Open a page below and print it: the masthead, the navigation and the buttons come off, and what is left is the content with its colours intact.

The whole table, on one sheet

Four colourings of all 118 elements. Three of them print on a single page at both A4 and Letter, with no column cut off and nothing left to a second sheet; the fourth carries a timeline of thirty decades below the table and runs onto a second. Each card says which.

Element cards

Every element has a page that prints as a card: the name, the symbol, the position, the properties the record holds and what the element is used for, with the diagrams and the navigation left off. Pick a group to print a set of them together, or the index for all 118.
